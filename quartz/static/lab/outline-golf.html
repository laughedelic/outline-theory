Outline Golf

Each hole gives you an outline, a target outline and a set of keys. Get from one to the other in as few presses as you can. Par is the fewest presses any run of those keys can take, found by searching every arrangement of the bullets. Afterwards each hole shows what the same job costs under other sets of keys, which is the question decision 18 puts to the plugin.

Every par comes from the same model as Keys to Anywhere: outlines of up to five labelled bullets, and keys that each move one bullet with what is under it. It is not the plugin’s code, but for today’s four keys the plugin’s own code gives the same outline as the model on every key press at three, four and five bullets measured (see Where It Lands). Outlines are drawn as bullets, two steps of indentation per level.

Play it

Pick a bullet, then press a key. The bullet keeps its colour, so you can follow it. A check mark means a bullet already sits where the target has it. A press that does nothing is not counted, and the message says why: that refusal is part of what the holes are about.

what the holes show

The same job under different keys

  • A first child cannot move up today. Hole 4 asks for a swap that takes – presses with today’s keys and – with a move up that leaves its list, the change issue #223 asks for. measured
  • The two outdents differ in presses. Taking b out of a list with two more bullets after it takes – press when outdent takes the later bullets with it (foundation law O11) and – when it moves b alone. measured Which one the plugin has is the foundation’s statement for outdent; this hole only counts presses. reported
  • “To top” and “to bottom” pay off on one kind of job. Reversing a list of five takes – presses with today’s keys, – when the keys leave the list and – with “to top” and “to bottom”. Across all one-bullet moves of five bullets they help less than leaving the list does (see Keys to Anywhere). measured
  • A set of keys without a way out is a trap. With Tab, up and down alone, hole 5 has no solution at all, and Keys to Anywhere shows that every set that reaches every outline from every other contains indent and a key that can take a bullet out of its parent. measured

What this suggests, and what it does not

  • Par is a cost in presses, not in surprise. Decision 18 weighs a key that changes at a list’s end against an addressed command; a puzzle can show the saving in presses, and cannot show what the changed key costs a user who expected it to do nothing. inferred
  • The game doubles as a check of the rule. A hole whose par with the plugin’s keys differs from par in this model is a place where the model’s key does not match the plugin’s, which is what the foundation’s planned reachability check (N11) would find on the real operations. inferred
  • Holes could come from real edits. The library cites observed edits that change containment (Ko, Aung and Myers, 2005); a course made of such edits would count presses on the jobs people do, not on random pairs. inferred

Method. reach/reach.js builds every labelled outline of n bullets and a table of edges for each key; the page searches breadth-first from the start outline over the keys of the chosen set, and a hole’s par is the distance to the target, or “no run” when the target is not reached. The model’s keys are listed in Keys to Anywhere. A random hole picks a start and a target at a distance in the chosen band under the chosen keys.

Limits. Outlines are forests of bullets only: no text, folds, headings or selections. A key acts on one chosen bullet; par does not count choosing a bullet, which a user also does. Outdent takes the later bullets with it unless the set says otherwise.
