A research series on what it takes to edit an outline that is stored as plain text: which keys are enough, what a write has to keep, and what happens when two devices edit the same note.

Each post takes one question and answers it as far as the evidence goes: a count on a small model, a measurement, or what the papers say. Most posts carry a figure that can be tried in the page.

The series is by [laughedelic](https://github.com/laughedelic) and grew out of the work on [True Outliner](https://github.com/laughedelic/obsidian-true-outliner), an outliner plugin for Obsidian.
